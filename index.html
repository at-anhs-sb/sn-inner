A message from the village

Welcome, warrior.

You've officially entered the Shonen Nexus. Before you wander into the forest and accidentally start a boss fight, here's what you should know.

01 — Respect your comrades. Everyone is here to enjoy anime, chess, competition, and the community. Don't ruin somebody else's arc.
02 — Play fair. No cheating, no exploiting, no nonsense. Win because you're good, not because you found a loophole.
03 — Keep the debates fun. Anime arguments are encouraged. Personal attacks are not. There's a difference between "your take is terrible" and actually being terrible to someone.
04 — Represent the Nexus. When you're competing under the Shonen Nexus banner, play with the same respect you'd expect from everyone else.
05 — Have fun. This is a community, not a second job. Play games. Make friends. Talk anime. Enjoy the arc.
